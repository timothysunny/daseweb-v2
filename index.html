Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start earning with DaseWeb?

To start earning, simply create a free account, and you’ll get access to all available tasks. Complete tasks such as liking posts, following profiles, and watching videos to earn real money.

How does payment work?

You can withdraw your earnings directly to your bank account or preferred payment method. We process payments quickly after your tasks are verified.

What types of tasks are available?

You will find a wide range of tasks, including following social media profiles, liking posts, commenting, watching videos, and more.

How do I know if a task is verified?

Once you complete a task, it will be reviewed, and if it meets the required standards, it will be verified. You will receive credit once a task is verified.

How do I post a task as an advertiser?

To post an ad you need to sign up for an advertiser account, and once verified you'll have access to the advertiser dashboard where you can create your task.

Is there any support if I have any issue?

Yes, if you have any issue, you can contact support and they'll try to assist you with your issue.
